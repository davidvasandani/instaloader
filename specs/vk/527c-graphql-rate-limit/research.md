Observed 2026-11-24: HTTP 200 payload {"errors":[{"message":"Rate limit exceeded","severity":"CRITICAL","code":1675004}],"extensions":{"is_final":true}} from a public post query. Instaloader 4.16 and upstream 7acd1b6 inspect absent metadata items and raise a generic error. get_json already handles TooManyRequestsException, but its recursive retry loses use_post. Upstream report: https://github.com/instaloader/instaloader/issues/2806. This change addresses the demonstrated throttle response, not every possible missing-metadata cause.
